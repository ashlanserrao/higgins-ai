# Idea ratings

Write y or n after each criterion, and one line after reason (most useful when something is n).
An idea passes only when all three are y. Rate before looking at the verdicts files.

- specific: names something concrete to make, do, serve or set up: a dish, an item, an activity, or for a theme a clear look (named colours, motifs or materials). It needs no quantities, equipment or steps. It fails only when it names nothing concrete ("beautiful decorations", "a lavish spread") or only restates the theme or a must-have.
- fits: suits this event type and occasion, its guests, city and customs, and the organiser's preferences: food should be what is usual for the occasion (a festival's traditional sweets and snacks, say), and the look should match the style asked for (a period or traditional style rules out a clashing modern one). It breaks no must-have (for example, no non-Jain dishes when Jain food only is a must-have). An idea need not deliver a must-have itself.
- doable: realistic for the budget level, the venue type, the guest count and the days left, with materials and ingredients that are easy to find in that city. A "tight" or "modest" budget needs low-cost, DIY-friendly ideas.
