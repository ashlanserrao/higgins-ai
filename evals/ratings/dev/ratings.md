# Idea ratings

Write y or n after each criterion, and one line after reason (most useful when something is n).
An idea passes only when all three are y. Rate before looking at verdicts.jsonl.

- specific: concrete enough to act on: what to make, do, serve or set up. Generic ideas ("beautiful decorations", "delicious food") or ones that only restate the theme fail.
- fits: suits this event type, its guests, city and customs, and the organiser's preferences, and breaks no must-have (for example, no non-Jain dishes when Jain food only is a must-have). An idea need not deliver a must-have itself.
- doable: realistic for the budget level, the venue type, the guest count and the days left. A "tight" or "modest" budget needs low-cost, DIY-friendly ideas.
